SYNTHETIC HISTORY

GB means Game Bits: invented in-game currency, not money. These two equal, fictional one-hour windows are a case file, not measurements of real players.

Store opens, rendered offers, buyers and crashes count distinct session traces. Spend sums successful purchases. These funnels come from the same synthetic traces you can inspect below.

Your incident brief: establish the affected scope, reproduce safely, follow the evidence, propose a mitigation or fix, then explain how you would verify recovery.
